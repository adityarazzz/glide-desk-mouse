Training

Record a short guided session. Each step shows the movement, waits until you are ready and tells you if it was seen. Glide is tuned on these recordings. Only hand positions are saved, never camera images.

Pointing testThe standard test for pointing devices (ISO 9241-411): four rounds of nine clicks. Run it with Glide, then with your mouse or touchpad, to compare them.
